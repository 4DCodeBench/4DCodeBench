4DCodeBench Evaluation Metrics

Scoring Protocol & Formulations

Full definitions: docs/spec.md. Every metric lies in [0, 1], higher is better, except Depth error, which lies in [0, 2], lower is better. The main metrics form five families: Perceptual, 2D Dynamics and 2.5D Geometry against the reference video; 3D Geometry and 3D Dynamics against the reference world.

Gates and states

Video gate: render.mp4 decodes and matches the reference resolution, frame count and frame rate. On failure every metric reads error.

World gate: every world entry and solution/build.sh exist, and the checker reports no violation. On failure the geometry-dependent metrics read error; DINOv3, TIPS and GeoPhys are still computed.

not_applicable: the metric does not apply to this kind of scene. excluded: the main object is transparent (six real scenes) and the metric compares rasterised geometry with the video. uncomputable: the reference lacks the signal the metric needs.

All scenes

DINOv3 similarity (Perceptual): frame-wise cosine of class-token embeddings.

Dynamic IoU (2D Dynamics): dynamic-object masks, a thin band along the reference boundary ignored.

Real videos Real

The reference side is estimated from the video; the reconstruction side is computed analytically from the 4D world.

Depth error (2.5D Geometry): Video Depth Anything disparity against the rasterised disparity, each median/MAD-normalised, the largest 1% of residuals discarded; uncovered pixels count as 1.

Flow (2D Dynamics): RAFT flow against analytic flow, compared per step as unordered sets.

Track2D (2D Dynamics): CoTracker3 paths against the reconstruction's surface points followed from the same queries; per-step DTW distance in image diagonals, capped at .

Synthetic scenes Synthetic

Registration: two similarity transforms fitted by trimmed ICP, on the frame-0 visible surfaces and on the dynamic surfaces over 16 frames; the one closer to the reference through the reference camera is kept. Distances are in reference RMS radii.

Scene 3D (3D Geometry): capped symmetric Chamfer distance of the frame-0 surfaces, .

Trajectory DTW (3D Dynamics, Lagrangian): sampled 3D paths matched one to one by the Hungarian algorithm on DTW distance, capped at 1.

EMD step (3D Dynamics, Eulerian): one-step 3D displacements compared as unordered sets.

Additional metrics

TIPS similarity: DINOv3 similarity with TIPSv2 embeddings.

GeoPhys DINOv3: five regularity descriptors of each video's DINOv3 feature trajectory, .

Uni3D point Synthetic: Uni3D cosine of the registered frame-0 clouds, mapped to [0, 1].

Uni3D MoGe Real: Uni3D cosine of the reconstruction's frame-0 surface and the MoGe-3 point map, mapped to [0, 1].

Occupancy DTW Synthetic: both worlds' matter at 16 frames as point sets, compared by capped sliced Wasserstein distance and time-warped.

Geometry quality

Over the runs that pass the world gate: the fraction of mesh components that are watertight, manifold, non-degenerate and free of self-intersections, and no interpenetration (one minus the fraction of vertices inside another object).
